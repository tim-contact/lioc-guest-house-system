# Meals

Operations users can record a meal or drink for a specific guest on a date between the reservation's arrival and departure, inclusive. The guest must be attached to the reservation or be its booker. The system cancellation/no-show status and conflicting reservations are excluded. Quantities are whole numbers from 1 to 100. A guest can have one active order per menu item and date; cancel and re-enter an order to correct it before billing.

The register can be filtered by date and guest house. It shows each guest's saved dietary preference and notes, daily quantities and price subtotals, and billing status. Cancelled orders and unbilled orders whose reservation has changed are excluded from totals. A meal keeps its recorded guest-house, item name, service, price, currency and tax treatment even if the menu is later edited. Guest-house reassignment, date changes, removal of the guest or system cancellation can invalidate an unbilled order; staff must review and cancel it before invoicing.

Administrators manage meal options under the menu on the same page. Each option has a name, service, price, currency and tax rate/treatment. Deactivating an option prevents new orders but retains history. The currency must match the application's invoice currency, and tax treatment must be configured before billing. Sri Lankan tax-exempt versus zero-rated legal classification and invoice template wording must be checked separately with finance.

When staff create an invoice from selected reservations, uninvoiced meals are added as separate miscellaneous lines using their recorded prices. All guests on a reservation are included on its invoice. Staff can remove a meal line from the draft to defer it; they cannot edit its price or quantity there. Final submission locks and rechecks each source order, atomically associates it with one invoice line, and rejects stale, cancelled, changed or already-invoiced orders. If a meal was added after an invoice draft was opened, restart invoice creation to include it.

Invoices containing meal lines cannot currently be deleted; the linked meal lines cannot be edited or deleted, and an invoiced meal cannot be cancelled. A formal invoice correction or credit-note workflow is needed before such changes can be made. Reservations with meal history cannot be deleted. Customer deletion reassigns meal history to the existing anonymous guest; guest GDPR exports include meal history. Meal-order audit values are redacted.
